# Healing and food

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

How should players recover health, and what role should food play, so that healing matters through the whole game without encroaching on brewing? Vanilla is the baseline to beat.

## Comments

From [Getting villagers and removing villages](08-villagers-and-villages.md):

- Plain golden apples lose their effects and become a base, like an unenchanted pickaxe. Decide their food value.
- An enchanted golden apple is made at the enchanting table and carries effects chosen like enchantments, one Enchantment Slot each, at 1 lapis per effect plus a block of gold on the first save. Regeneration II unlocks at 15% Library Progress. Decide the other effects (Fire Resistance, Absorption and Resistance were suggested, maybe more), their strengths and durations, and when each unlocks. The default is a Library Progress threshold per effect. The alternative is tying an effect to a shelved enchantment, such as Fire Resistance to Fire Protection IV.
- With a gold farm, these apples are renewable, so weigh them against brewing, as the Matcha manifesto warns.

## Answer

Keep vanilla for now. Hunger, saturation, natural regeneration and food values all stay as they are, and no new healing system is added. We didn't find a design that beats vanilla, so this can be reopened if a good idea turns up.

- A plain golden apple keeps vanilla's food value and loses its effects, as [Getting villagers and removing villages](08-villagers-and-villages.md) decided.
- The enchanted golden apple's effects aren't a food question, so they moved to [Brewing](11-brewing.md).
