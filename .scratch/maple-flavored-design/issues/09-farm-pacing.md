# Farm pacing

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05, 08

## Question

When should iron farms and mob farms become possible, and what gates them, so that they're a reward but not a day 1 build?

## Answer

No new gates are needed, since other decisions already pace farms.

- **Iron farms** need villagers, and [Getting villagers and removing villages](08-villagers-and-villages.md) sets that path: no golems spawn in villages, and every villager is cured, which takes Weakness from the Nether, an enchanted golden apple, 15% Library Progress and level 30. Raid farms need villagers too, so they wait for the same point.
- **Other farms** stay vanilla. Zombies already jump and pathfind, so no common design depends on one-block walls, and stepping up a full block doesn't change them.
- **Zombie health** only matters to farms that pre-damage mobs with a fall for a player kill. Those are uncommon, mostly Skyblock iron ingot farms, and a shorter drop fixes them.
- **Creeper farms** must sit below Y 63, as [Mob changes](05-mob-changes.md) decided.

These come from reasoning and play experience. None of it has been tested in a world.

## Comments

From [Getting villagers and removing villages](08-villagers-and-villages.md):

- Villages are abandoned and have no iron golems. Every villager is earned by curing, which needs Weakness (from the Nether), and an enchanted golden apple (15% Library Progress and level 30). Breeding stays vanilla after that, so an iron farm is possible from about that point unless this ticket gates golems further.

## Comments

From [Mob changes](05-mob-changes.md):

- Adult zombies step up a full block on Normal and Hard, so check common mob farm designs against that.
- Creepers don't spawn naturally at Y 63 or above, so gunpowder farms using creepers must sit below sea level.
