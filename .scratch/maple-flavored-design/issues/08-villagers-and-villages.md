# Getting villagers and removing villages

Type: grilling
Blocked by: 07
Status: resolved

## Question

How do villages go away, and how do players earn their first villagers, so that finding a village can't skip progression?

## Answer

Finding a village no longer skips progression. Every village is abandoned, and a villager is earned by curing a zombie villager with an enchanted golden apple, which takes the Nether, a Library and level 30. Terms are in `GLOSSARY.md`. Numbers are starting values that balance work can change. The rules are fixed. Research: [Matcha Flavoured's villages](../../../docs/research/matcha-villagers.md).

### Villages

- Every village generates as an Abandoned Village: its villagers are zombie villagers, and it has no iron golems.
- Villages are much rarer, at spacing 80 and separation 50, about 5.5 times rarer than vanilla. These numbers come from Matcha Flavoured.
- Hay bales are removed, both the piles and the hay in barns, so one village no longer gives stacks of bread.
- Every brewing stand in village buildings is removed, including the church's.
- Crops, beds, bells and the other workstations stay. They're a slow farm start, and what the cured villagers will need.
- Village chests never hold diamonds, obsidian or iron gear. Their exact contents belong to Structures.

### Earning villagers

Curing is the only way to get a villager. It takes a splash potion of Weakness and an enchanted golden apple.

- The igloo basement's villager becomes a second zombie villager, so no structure hands out a living villager.
- Naturally spawning zombie villagers and villagers converted by zombies stay vanilla, so a player who never finds a village can still earn villagers.
- Once you have villagers, breeding, workstations and professions stay vanilla.

**Weakness** is brewed only from Strength, which needs the Nether.

- A fermented spider eye on Strength gives Weakness (1:30). On Long Strength it gives Long Weakness (4:00), and on Strength II it gives Weakness.
- A fermented spider eye on a water bottle does nothing.
- Splash and lingering versions are vanilla.
- Witches still throw Weakness, which advanced players can use, as in skyblock.

**Enchanted golden apples** are made at the enchanting table, like gear.

- A plain golden apple is the base and has no effects, just food, like an unenchanted pickaxe. It keeps vanilla's food value.
- An enchanted golden apple is a golden apple carrying one or more effects. The table offers effects the way it offers enchantments, and each effect takes an Enchantment Slot. Gold's extra Enchantment Slot doesn't apply, because that's for tools and armor.
- Saving costs 1 lapis per effect, and the first save also takes a block of gold as the material unit. The grindstone removes the effects and returns the golden apple and the block of gold, but not the lapis.
- Regeneration II is the first effect, unlocked at 15% Library Progress. Further effects unlock as Library Progress grows, and once unlocked, any combination can be chosen. No effect is required.
- So the first enchanted golden apple needs 15% Library Progress and level 30.
- Any enchanted golden apple cures a zombie villager, whatever its effects.
- Every enchanted golden apple in a loot table becomes a plain golden apple, so the table is the only source.

**Library Progress** is shown on the table's progress bar. It's the sum of the highest level shelved for each enchantment, over a Full Library's 130. Shelving Sharpness I and II counts 2, and two Sharpness I books count 1. A Full Library is 100%, which offers Mending. At 15%, the apple needs 20 levels, about a dozen structures' books.

So a player's first villagers come after the Nether, a modest Library and level 30. That's early enough for the Master Armorer's Reinforcement template to arrive before Protection.

### Trades

- No villager sells Crystal Hearts or Pith.
- No villager sells raw diamonds, as in vanilla, so Crystal Heart crafting keeps its limit.
- Master Armorers stay reachable through curing, so Reinforcement stays reachable.
- Toolsmiths sell copper tools instead of stone ones, as Early-game tiers decided.
- Cure discounts and trades of enchanted gear stay with trading economy details, still in the map's fog.

## Comments

From Hearts and death:

- Should villagers trade Crystal Hearts? Matcha's book trades aren't farmable only if the books are non-renewable, and Matcha also puts Divine Comedy and Paradise Lost in fishing treasure.
- Crystal Heart crafting is limited by diamonds being non-renewable, because Evokers make Pith farmable. No trade should provide diamonds without accounting for this.

From [Early-game tiers](07-early-game-tiers.md):

- Master Armorers sell the Reinforcement template, and it appears in no chest loot, so Armorers must stay reachable. Vanilla's chainmail trades stay.
- Toolsmiths that sold stone tools sell the copper versions instead.
