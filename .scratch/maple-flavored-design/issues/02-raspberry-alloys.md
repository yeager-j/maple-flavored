# How Raspberry Flavoured designs alloys and progression

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

How does Raspberry Flavoured design its alloys, materials and progression, including Divine Fragments? Which ideas could inform an alloy system distinct from Matcha's?

## Answer

From the pack's files and public pages, not from playing it. Full findings, including nine options for a system without Create: `research/raspberry-alloys.md` on branch `research/raspberry-alloys`.

- Divine Fragments are Matcha's item, not Raspberry's. The closest equivalents are probably Stardust (crushed Nether Star) and Soul Beads (destroyed spawners), and neither goes into alloys. This is an inference.
- Raspberry also hosts alloys on base materials: Rose Gold and Steel on iron are stated, while Electrum, Necromium and Netherite on diamond are inferred.
- Its distinctive ideas are heated then superheated mixing, material traits that replace removed enchantments, alloy uses beyond gear, per-alloy weapons, and durability that isn't tied to tier (Steel beats diamond).

Unconfirmed: exact gear smithing recipes and in-game behaviour. CurseForge refused direct requests, so its page wasn't fully read.
