# Trading, combat and recipes

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

The map's last fog: what's left to decide about Nether and End progression, trading, armor and combat, and recipes?

## Answer

Little beyond what other tickets decided. Everything not listed here stays vanilla.

### Nether and End progression

Settled by the existing tickets. Deeper End changes belong to a separate End Update mod, an expansion that requires this one.

### Trading

- Villagers sell plain gear wherever vanilla sells enchanted gear, including tools, weapons, armor, bows and fishing rods. This matches [Enchanting](10-enchanting.md)'s rule that found gear is never enchanted.
- Librarians buy books instead of selling them, as Enchanting decided.
- Everything else stays vanilla, including cure discounts.

### Armor and combat

Vanilla apart from Reinforcement, which [Early-game tiers](07-early-game-tiers.md) decided.

### Recipes

- A Crystal Heart is 8 diamonds around 1 Pith, as [Hearts and death](03-hearts-and-death.md) decided. The recipe comes from Matcha Flavoured, with credit.
- A Nether Star crafts into 9 Pith, as in Matcha Flavoured, and keeps its vanilla name. There's no recipe the other way. A Wither farm that works despite the Wither's Subspace earns its Pith.
- The Alloy Furnace mirrors the blast furnace recipe: 5 gold ingots across the top and sides, a blast furnace in the centre, and 3 polished blackstone bricks along the bottom.
- Each Alloy has a storage block: 9 ingots make a block, and a block crafts back into 9 ingots. Alloy blocks work as beacon base blocks, like blocks of Netherite.

### Enchanting and repair materials

For items with no ingot, enchanting and repair use the vanilla repair item where one exists, and the main crafting ingredient otherwise:

| Item | Material |
| --- | --- |
| Elytra | Phantom membrane |
| Mace | Breeze rod |
| Bow, crossbow, fishing rod | String |
| Trident | Prismarine shard |
| Shears, flint and steel | Iron ingot |

This closes Enchanting's open items. Rewards on the progress bar before Mending are the enchanted golden apple's effects, which [Brewing](11-brewing.md) set.
