# Storage and End City rewards

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 06

## Question

What makes End Cities worth exploring in full, what should their loot be, and what are the storage options before and after shulker boxes?

## Answer

Storage stays vanilla, and End Cities change only through their loot. Deeper End changes belong to a separate End Update mod. Numbers are starting values that balance work can change.

### Storage

Bundles, Ender Chests and shulker boxes stay vanilla, and nothing is added. Shulker farms also stay vanilla, and [Blood Moons](15-blood-moons.md) still Restore shulkers.

### End Cities

Structures, mobs, chests and the End Ship stay vanilla, including the guaranteed Elytra. The Feather Falling book stays where [Enchanting](10-enchanting.md) placed it, and found gear in chests is plain, as Enchanting decided.

Each End City chest gets two new, separate rolls, to make up for the lost enchantments:

| Item | Chance per chest | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pith | 50% | 1 or 2 |
| Crystal Heart | 15% | 1 |

Chests don't refill, so this supply is finite. These odds are our own, because Matcha Flavoured doesn't put Crystal Hearts in End City chests.

Players can still reach chests by breaking in from outside. That's accepted for now.

### Ideas for the End Update mod

These were discussed but not decided, and they're not part of this mod:

- End City chests become vaults guarded by trial spawners, so a threat can't be skipped by breaking a wall. Shulkers stay as a hazard, because End Cities aren't very dangerous and Bastions are much scarier.
- The Elytra comes from an ominous vault on the End Ship, at odds similar to the heavy core (10% in vanilla's ominous vault), perhaps guaranteed after 5 ships.
- End Cities use their own keys, so Trial Chamber keys can't skip the fights.
- Spawners summon Endermen, Endermites and Phantoms.
