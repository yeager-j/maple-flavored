# 35: Alloy set bonuses

**What to build:** A full set of one Alloy gives a strong bonus of its own.

**Blocked by:** 07 (Crystal Heart, Pith and Netherite recipe), 08 (Withered Hearts), 33 (Alloy gear and numeric traits)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

Spec: the Maple Flavored spec in this folder.

- [ ] Steel: knockback and Levitation immunity. Electrum: fire and lava immunity, Withered Hearts every 10 seconds, changing as soon as the set changes. Shakudo: no slowdown from soul sand, honey, cobwebs or powder snow. Adamant: 25% of melee damage ignores armor points, not Protection
- [ ] Orichalcum: lethal-hit save with Totem effects, before a held Totem or Crystal Heart, off in Hardcore. It starts ready; after a save a timer on the player recharges it over 30 minutes of online play, worn or not; death doesn't reset it
- [ ] Bonuses update on equipment change
- [ ] Advancement: Wear a full set of each Alloy, at any time (Nether challenge, 100 XP, under Smelt an Alloy ingot)
- [ ] GameTests cover each bonus, the save order and the recharge
