# 32: Alloy Furnace and Alloy materials

**What to build:** Piglin Brutes drop Ancient Embers, and the Alloy Furnace turns a Netherite Ingot and a storage block into an Alloy ingot.

**Blocked by:** 07 (Crystal Heart, Pith and Netherite recipe)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

Spec: the Maple Flavored spec in this folder.

- [ ] Ancient Ember item; Piglin Brutes drop it at 25% plus 5% per Looting level, killed by a player, separate from Pith
- [ ] Five Alloy ingots (Steel iron, Electrum gold, Shakudo copper, Adamant diamond, Orichalcum emerald) and five Alloy blocks (9 each way, beacon base)
- [ ] Alloy Furnace block, menu and recipe type: 1 Netherite Ingot + 1 storage block, 1 Ember per ingot, 10 seconds; hoppers work; crafted from 5 gold ingots, a blast furnace and 3 polished blackstone bricks
- [ ] Any furnace smelts an Alloy ingot back into a Netherite Ingot
- [ ] Advancements: Get an Ancient Ember (Nether, under Those Were the Days), Smelt an Alloy ingot (under it), But why? (hidden challenge, 100 XP, under Beaconator: a level 4 beacon on Alloy blocks only)
- [ ] GameTests cover drops, the furnace with hoppers, smelting back and the beacon
